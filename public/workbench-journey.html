01 / ORGANIZE AROUND THE WORK

Tenants become Projects

The Tenant concept being added to Workbench becomes a work-centric Project, not a user-centric container. Project-level artifacts give context and outputs a home that spans Threads.

02 / CONTEXT WITHOUT THE BACKLOG

No bookkeeping

Task bookkeeping is gone: no statuses to maintain or backlog to groom. Recent keeps updated Threads in view and automatically archives older ones out of the way. Put a Thread to sleep when it can wait; an update or a deliberate wake brings it back, with its context intact.

03 / ROOM FOR THE SIGNAL

Recognizable From a Distance

Cards with generated icons make Threads easy to recognize and leave room for changing progress and Impact widgets. The card grid offers the right zoom level for managing parallel work with minimum effort: scan progress, spot what needs attention, and steer agents without opening each Thread.

A grid with the work close at hand. One Project fills the workspace with Thread cards. Thread details float beside their card; Project context opens from a fixed bottom dock. Curated files are Artifacts. Files on Comments and messages are Attachments.
