01 / ORGANIZE AROUND THE WORK

Tenants become Projects

The Tenant concept being added to Workbench becomes a work-centric Project, not a user-centric container. Project-level artifacts give context and outputs a home that spans Threads.

02 / CONTEXT WITHOUT THE BACKLOG

Threads, not Tasks

A Thread can hold a subject or long-running work without becoming an endless task backlog. Incomplete work, unread updates, and sleep are independent signals, not one Status. Requests come and go; older Threads leave the default view.

03 / ROOM FOR THE SIGNAL

Recognizable From a Distance

Cards with generated icons make Threads easy to recognize and leave room for changing progress and Impact widgets. Automatically filtering older Threads reduces the need for compact lists that compete with the work for horizontal space.

07 / Mobile experience

Three columns. Three screens.

Items, Work, and Artifact preview become full-width screens. Your private Agent Channel floats above the current screen when you tap Agent.

Left → ItemsCenter → WorkRight → PreviewAgent → Floating conversation

Touch replaces hover. A compact header provides Back, the current Project, and an Agent button with activity. The screen switcher follows the three content columns. Opening an Attachment shows Preview; Expand enters Artifact context. The Agent Channel is never a pinned screen or column.

Context travels with youSwitching screens preserves the selected Thread, scroll positions, answers, and drafts. Expanding a preview replaces Work with Artifact content and switches Items to peers. Back restores the Thread; dismissing Agent simply reveals the screen underneath.
Touch and keyboardControls have at least 44px touch targets. Pick files or photos rather than relying on drag-and-drop. Image selection would use an explicit Mark region mode; this study demonstrates a preselected region and expandable annotation.

Feature explainers / Outside the journey

More context. Less hunting.

These are working feature studies, not later steps in the story. Floating surfaces are shown open below so the ideas are visible without discovering every hover.

Your work, or a read-only view through someone else's eyes.

Select a member to see the Threads they created, were assigned, or edited, using their own sleep preferences. Search overrides all filters. Other-member views are live and read-only, outlined in yellow. In your own view, only the assigned user can edit a Thread; new Threads are assigned to the owner.

Activity that you can follow into the work.

The titlebar shows the busiest three members over 24 hours. The open vertical view below shows up to 15. Select a user, then a Thread icon; hover a white bar for the work behind that activity. Change the time span or go Back without losing your place.

A familiar shape for every subject.

Project and Thread icons are generated monochrome SVGs. Hover an icon to regenerate it with optional guidance. This local study generates deterministic SVG variations, not a real AI request.

Peek before changing your view.

Hover an unselected member for their recently updated Threads. This is the same live list selecting that member would show, not a separate activity feed.

Gutters are the panel, compressed.

Each mark aligns with the element it represents, including while the panel is hidden. The top gutter stays visible when the titlebar opens.
