01 / ORGANIZE AROUND THE WORK

Tenants become Projects

The Tenant concept being added to Workbench becomes a work-centric Project, not a user-centric container. Project-level artifacts give context and outputs a home that spans Threads.

02 / CONTEXT WITHOUT THE BACKLOG

Threads, not Tasks

A Thread can hold a subject or long-running work without becoming an endless task backlog. Incomplete work, unread updates, and sleep are independent signals, not one Status. Requests come and go; older Threads leave the default view.

03 / ROOM FOR THE SIGNAL

Recognizable From a Distance

Cards with generated icons make Threads easy to recognize and leave room for changing progress and Impact widgets. Automatically filtering older Threads reduces the need for compact lists that compete with the work for horizontal space.

A grid with the work close at hand. One Project fills the workspace with Thread cards. Thread details float beside their card; Project context opens from a fixed bottom dock. Curated files are Artifacts. Files on Comments and messages are Attachments.
